Martin Hernandez

Postdoctoral Scholar · Department of Statistics & Data Science · UCLA

I am an applied mathematician working at the interface of control theory, reinforcement learning, numerical analysis, and machine learning. My current research focuses on continuous-time reinforcement learning and mean-field control, with broader interests in randomized numerical methods, partial differential equations, and the mathematical foundations of learning.

Research

Mathematical methods for learning and control in continuous time.

My work combines analysis, numerical methods, and learning-based approaches, with an emphasis on mathematically structured models and algorithms.

Continuous-time reinforcement learning

02

Randomized numerical methods

Neural network approximation
